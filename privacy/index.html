Privacy on this website.

A short description of this public site and the services it links to.

Updated October 24, 2026. This page describes the website in this revision. It is separate from the proposed privacy features of future Kaskade tools.

Reading the site

The current pages contain no account system, payment flow, chat import, or live AI processing. Fonts are served with the site. The site code contains no analytics or advertising scripts.

The hosting service still receives ordinary web requests and may process connection information under its own policies. This page does not claim anonymous or unlogged browsing.

The waitlist

The waitlist link opens an external Google Form. At the time of this review, it requests an email address. Google handles the form service under its own terms and privacy information.

Email and other links

Email you send to kaskade.evolution@gmail.com is handled through the email service. Please send only the information needed for your question; avoid including sensitive personal material.

External sites, including GitHub and any future social profiles, have their own policies. Links here do not make those services part of Kaskade.

Questions

For a question about information you have submitted, contact the project by email.
