# Assay Office — a Kaskade pilot proposal

## Implemented

Static page at `/assay/`, inline CSS and JavaScript, self-hosted fonts, and an email-drafting form. Contact: `kaskade.evolution@gmail.com`. The form composes a message in the visitor's mail app; it does not send mail, upload files, or store a submission.

The optional publication-interest checkbox is off by default. Either branch requires separate publication approval. The source includes shared social metadata, a canonical URL, and public indexing metadata; search-engine indexing is not guaranteed.

## Prototype

The only completed test is an offline deterministic 50-case synthetic benchmark. The recorded checker flagged 25/25 corrupted reports and accepted 25/25 clean reports at each tier. A complete fictional registry made every corrupted report discoverable by the basic checks. These sample counts do not establish production accuracy, semantic judging, customer value, or live arbitration.

The public section summarizes the internal recorded run; raw fixtures, answer keys, checker code, and private methodology are not published here. The report specimens and replay animation are fictional examples, not live checks or customer results.

## Proposal

The service invitation covers up to 25 lead entries or 15 cited claims, tool-assisted and human-reviewed. Scope, reviewer, delivery date, tool use, and retention/deletion terms must be agreed before files are shared. The three-business-day turnaround is a target to confirm, not an automated service guarantee. Publication requires separate approval and is not a condition of a free pilot.

Freshness checks compare dated evidence with agreed cutoffs. Duplicate checks cover supplied material; no blanket conclusion about previous resale is made. Full source archiving, signed certificates, cross-provider judging, binding disputes, and payment/reputation integrations are not claimed as implemented services.

The working presentation is Assay Office by Kaskade. The dated GPT/Architect Annotation records the naming proposal and current status. No canonical naming decision is declared by this branch.

## Metaphor

Gold, pyrite, streak tests, fire assays, and umpire assays are analogies. They do not claim accreditation or certification of truth, conversion, or commercial value.
