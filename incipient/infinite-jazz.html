How a lineage grows
The rules of the room
Root
A finished release with its source intact. Hash-locked. Never edited after the fact.
Annotation
Anything that grows from a root: a take, a remix, a reply. It carries a date, a name and a note on what changed.
Promotion
Only Graham decides what becomes the next root. Until then an annotation is a branch, not the trunk.
Registry
Every root and annotation has an entry: title, lineage, hash and date. The registry is a small command-line tool kept with the music.

This is how Infinite Jazz can be infinite without becoming a pile. The instrument is the lineage itself: it can be played by one person over years, or by many people in a week, and the record of who did what stays legible.

The journal has the longer story of why the house works this way. Read it there.
