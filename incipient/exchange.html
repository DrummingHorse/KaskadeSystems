Listing an instrument

Describe it

What it is, what it sounds like, what's wrong with it. Photos in daylight. A price you'd accept today.

It gets read

Every listing is read by a person before it appears. Vague condition notes get sent back for another pass.

It goes on the board

Buyers reach you through the board. Payment and shipping are arranged between the two of you.

The board opens with real listings. Until then there is nothing to submit.
