About Incipient

Incipient Artistic Ventures is the umbrella for the music, writing and visual work of Graham Benjamin Higgins, and for the small ventures that grow around them: a store, a studio, an exchange. One mark, one palette, one rule.

Who makes the work
Graham Benjamin Higgins

Graham Benjamin Higgins is a composer, multi-instrumentalist and recording artist with thirty years of production behind him, across more genres than is sensible.

The music runs dark, intimate and psychological, with odd meters and an allergy to the obvious. He writes screenplays with the same temperament, builds his own websites by hand, and keeps the whole archive on the principle that a thing recorded once should be able to improve forever without losing its root. He lives and works in the Hudson Valley, New York.

The rooms, and what each one is for

Pure Audio

The listening room. Original music, finished and in progress, with previews and the story of how each piece was made. Bandcamp carries the releases until the store opens.

Infinite Jazz

The living instrument. An improvisation lineage where every release is a root and everything that grows from it stays attached and dated.

Pages

The writing desk. Screenplays, a novel with a graphic-novel bible, and the essay on authorship the house is built on.

Gallery

The wall. Visual work in the emblem's language, the portrait, and the mark.

Studio

Web design, built by hand, for musicians, writers and small ventures. Opens to outside clients when the house is finished.

Store

Releases to own, delivered directly as high-resolution files with a lineage note. In preview until the first files are uploaded.

Exchange

A board for instruments and studio gear, between people who play. Sample listings until the board opens.

Journal

Notes from the house, and the one door that opens onto the wider process behind the work.

Where things stand

This is an unlisted preview. The house is being reviewed room by room before the door is announced.
