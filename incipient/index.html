How the house works

Incipient is the room. Bandcamp is the store, for now. Perpetua is the vault. Infinite Jazz is the living instrument.

Each room keeps one kind of work legible: a title, a place, a path back to its source, and a reason to be here. The rooms share a mark, a palette and a rule: capture once, improve forever. The door is unlisted while the rooms are finished; this is the house being built with the lights on.
