Screenplays, a novel in progress, and the essay that explains how the house thinks about authorship. Everything here is in development and not yet published; the titles are real, the pages are being worked.

For the screen
Feature screenplay

The Moo Moo

A dark comedy. Jedd, thirty-eight, still sleeps with the cow-print blanket he has called Moo Moo since childhood. A therapist's line about setting boundaries lands wrong, people start dying peacefully, and his only friend Lenny has to decide where support ends and complicity begins. The characters never know they are in a comedy.

Complete draft
Feature screenplay, sequel

Moo Moo 2

A year later. The blanket came home. A dating-app encounter opens onto a nursery, a crib, and a grown man in it. The satire turns on reparenting, performative consent and a media machine that sells murder as content. Two further films are sketched: Moo Moo 3 and Moo Moo Forever.

Second draft
Mystery screenplay, also planned as a novella

Lauded, Pilloried, and Absconded

A mystery in five clocks. In 1887 on the Gold Coast, an Irish eccentric named Grahamuel Samuel O'Higgins is celebrated, condemned and declared missing by official notices dated in the wrong order. When his notebook resurfaces in 2026 as a self-editing file, his great-grand-step-descendant must work out whether time is fracturing, records are forging events, or two writers across centuries are inventing each other.

Essay, working paper

The Creative Loop

Authorship, agency and the recursive evolution of artistic production in the age of generative AI. The argument the whole house rests on: generating material is not the same as authoring it; authorship lives in constraint, selection, arrangement, revision and the decision to call something finished. Every contribution to the paper, by person or model, is on record.

Working paper
How the desk works

Each script has a desk. Suggestions arrive numbered, with their source named. The author reads them, decides, and the decision is written down beside the draft.

Nothing is rewritten silently. A draft stays the draft until Graham promotes a revision, and the earlier version stays on file next to it. It is the same rule the music keeps: capture once, improve forever, and never lose the root.

When a script is ready to be read by someone outside the house, it will be announced in the journal.

Incipient Artistic Ventures is the house for the music, writing and visual work of Graham Benjamin Higgins. Hudson Valley, New York.
