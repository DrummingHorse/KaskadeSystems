The way it's built

Written, not generated from a theme

Plain HTML and CSS you can read line by line. No framework to upgrade, no build step to break, no plugin that stops working next year.

Private by default

No trackers, no analytics scripts, no third-party fonts. A visitor's browser talks to your site and nobody else.

Yours to keep

You receive a folder. Put it on any host, hand it to another designer, or open it in twenty years. The site is the product, not the subscription.

How a site gets made

Brief

A conversation about what the site is for, what you have (photos, music, words) and what it should feel like.

Draft

A first version of the real pages, with your real content, rather than a mockup of someone else's.

Review

Two rounds of changes. You see every page at phone and desktop width before anything is final.

Hand-off

The folder, a short written guide to editing it, and help putting it on the host you choose.
