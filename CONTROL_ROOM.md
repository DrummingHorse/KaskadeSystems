## First decision: what should I do next?
1. **Check the operator's capacity and goal**: revenue evidence, shipping a complete artifact, rigorous research, or housekeeping. Never assume unlimited capacity.
2. **Read outstanding pull requests and open issues**; compare with merged changes before interpreting a stale issue as unfinished work.
3. **Select one bounded action** whose output and pass/fail evidence can be inspected in one session.
4. **Require the approval gate**: agents may investigate and propose; only the Operator promotes, publishes, merges, contacts outside parties, or commits resources.
5. **Report**: verified now / blocked / proposed next / links to supporting artifacts.

## Scoring / prioritization
Use only observed evidence; otherwise write `unknown`. Rate each candidate action on:
- Leverage: does this unblock other tasks or test a paid need?
- Evidence: can we verify its premise and outcome?
- Effort: can it be accomplished in one focused pass?
- Dependency: what must happen first?
- Reversibility/privacy: can it be safely rolled back, and does it expose anything?
Decide **one primary string**, one fallback, and one thing deliberately deferred. Don't invent numeric certainty or revenue forecasts.
