Proposal · The wider Kaskade project

Distinct roles.
One carried-forward piece of work.

Kaskade explores a structured way to develop work with AI: carry an artifact through distinct roles for analysis, expansion, refinement, critique, and synthesis. Each stage receives the previous work and its unresolved questions.
